Crispy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Bites

Rich, comforting, and super crispy, these bites are coated in garlic butter and tossed in warm Parmesan cheese to create a nutty, irresistible golden crust.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 4 cloves fresh garlic, minced
  • ⅓ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  • Coat:Toss chicken with cornstarch, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  • Sear:Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken in a single layer for 3–4 minutes, stir, and finish cooking for 4–5 minutes.
  • Butter & Cheese:Lower heat to medium-low. Add butter and garlic; cook for 1 minute. Turn off the heat completely, sprinkle in the Parmesan cheese, and toss quickly so it melts into a warm coating.
  • Serve:Sprinkle with parsley and serve.
