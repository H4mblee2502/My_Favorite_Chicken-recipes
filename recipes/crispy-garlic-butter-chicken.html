Crispy Garlic Butter Chicken

This crispy garlic butter chicken recipe features golden, crunchy chicken bites coated in rich, fragrant garlic butter sauce. Key ingredients include boneless chicken, cornstarch, and fresh butter. It takes 20 minutes to prepare.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt and ¼ teaspoon black pepp
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 5 cloves fresh garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  • Toss the chicken pieces in a bowl with cornstarch, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper until fully coated.
  • Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  • Add the chicken in a single layer and cook for 3 to 4 minutes without moving it to form a crust, then stir and cook for 5 more minutes until golden and done.
  • Reduce heat to medium-low, add the butter and minced garlic, and stir until the butter melts and smells amazing (about 1 minute).
  • Remove from heat, mix in the fresh parsley, and serve right away.
