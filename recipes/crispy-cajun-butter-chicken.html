Crispy Garlic Butter Chicken

Packed with bold Southern kick, this recipe features seared, spiced chicken coated in a rich, garlic-infused Cajun butter sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning (low sodium works best)
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oill
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 4 cloves fresh garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley or green onions, chopped

Instructions

  • Coat: Toss chicken with cornstarch, Cajun seasoning, and black pepper.
  • Sear: Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and sear for 3–4 minutes without moving, then stir and cook 4–5 minutes until done.
  • Cajun Butter: Reduce heat to medium-low. Melt butter in the skillet with minced garlic, stirring for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  • Serve:Remove from heat, toss with parsley, and serve immediately.
