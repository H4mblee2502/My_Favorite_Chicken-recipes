Crispy Lemon Herb Butter Chicken

Bright, zest-infused, and deeply savory, this light pan-seared chicken features fresh citrus and herbs that cut through rich garlic butter, making it perfect for pairing with pasta, rice, or veggies

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • ½ tsp dried oregano (or Italian seasoning)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 4 cloves fresh garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice (plus lemon zest)
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  • Coat:Toss chicken with cornstarch, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
  • Sear: Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add chicken in a single layer and cook for 3–4 minutes untouched to form a crust. Stir and cook 4–5 minutes more until done.
  • Sauce:Reduce heat to medium-low. Add butter, minced garlic, honey, and soy sauce. Stir for 1–2 minutes until the sauce bubbles and thickens around the chicken.
  • Serve:Garnish with fresh parsley or green onions and serve immediately.
