Crispy Sweet Chili Garlic Chicken

Inspired by favorite takeout flavors, this quick skillet meal delivers crunchy chicken pieces tossed in a glossy, sweet, and mildly spicy Thai chili glaze.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp vegetable or sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 cloves fresh garlic, minced
  • ⅓ cup sweet Thai chili sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sriracha (optional, for extra heat)
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds & chopped green onions for garnish

Instructions

  • Coat:Toss chicken with cornstarch, garlic powder, and salt.
  • Sear: Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Cook chicken pieces for 3–4 minutes untouched, then stir and cook 4–5 minutes more.
  • Sauce: Reduce heat to medium-low. Add butter and garlic, cooking for 30 seconds. Pour in sweet chili sauce, soy sauce, and optional sriracha. Stir continuously for 1–2 minutes until sticky.
  • Serve:Top with sesame seeds and green onions.
