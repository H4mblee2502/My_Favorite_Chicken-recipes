## 💡 Master Technique & Cooking Tips

To achieve the best crispiness and juicy interior, follow these core cooking principles:

1. **Pat the Chicken Dry:** Moisture prevents browning. Dry chicken breast thoroughly with paper towels before cutting and tossing in cornstarch.
2. **The Cornstarch Coating:** Cornstarch absorbs surface moisture and creates an ultra-thin, crispy barrier when seared in hot oil.
3. **Don't Overcrowd the Pan:** Cook in two batches if your skillet is under 12 inches. Overcrowding steams the chicken instead of searing it.
4. **Leave It Undisturbed:** Allow the chicken pieces to sear untouched for 3 to 4 minutes to lock in juice and create a deep golden crust before stirring.
5. **Add Butter & Garlic at Low Heat:** Lower the flame before introducing garlic and butter to prevent the garlic from burning and turning bitter.

## 🍽️ Recipes

### 1. Crispy Garlic Butter Chicken Bites
This *crispy garlic butter chicken recipe* features golden, crunchy chicken bites coated in rich, fragrant garlic butter sauce. Key ingredients include boneless chicken, cornstarch, and fresh butter. It takes 20 minutes to prepare.

* **Prep time:** 10 mins
* **Cook time:** 10 mins
* **Servings:** 3–4

#### Ingredients
* **1 lb** boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
* **2 tbsp** cornstarch
* **1 tsp** garlic powder
* **½ tsp** salt
* **¼ tsp** black pepper
* **1 tbsp** olive oil
* **4 tbsp** unsalted butter
* **5 cloves** fresh garlic, minced
* **1 tbsp** fresh parsley, chopped

#### Instructions
1. **Coat:** Toss the chicken pieces in a bowl with cornstarch, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper until fully coated.
2. **Heat:** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. **Sear:** Add the chicken in a single layer and cook for 3 to 4 minutes without moving it to form a crust, then stir and cook for 5 more minutes until golden and done.
4. **Butter Sauce:** Reduce heat to medium-low, add the butter and minced garlic, and stir until the butter melts and smells amazing (about 1 minute).
5. **Serve:** Remove from heat, mix in the fresh parsley, and serve right away.

### 2. Crispy Honey Garlic Chicken Bites
This crowd-pleasing dish pairs crispy, golden chicken with a sticky sauce that strikes the ultimate balance between sweet honey, savory soy, and aromatic garlic.

* **Prep time:** 10 mins
* **Cook time:** 10 mins
* **Servings:** 3–4

#### Ingredients
* **1 lb** boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
* **2 tbsp** cornstarch
* **½ tsp** garlic powder
* **½ tsp** salt
* **¼ tsp** black pepper
* **1 tbsp** olive oil
* **3 tbsp** unsalted butter
* **4 cloves** fresh garlic, minced
* **3 tbsp** honey
* **1 tbsp** soy sauce
* **1 tbsp** fresh parsley or green onions, chopped

#### Instructions
1. **Coat:** Toss chicken with cornstarch, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
2. **Sear:** Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add chicken in a single layer and cook for 3–4 minutes untouched to form a crust. Stir and cook 4–5 minutes more until done.
3. **Glaze:** Reduce heat to medium-low. Add butter, minced garlic, honey, and soy sauce. Stir for 1–2 minutes until the sauce bubbles and thickens around the chicken.
4. **Serve:** Garnish with fresh parsley or green onions and serve immediately.

### 3. Crispy Lemon Herb Butter Chicken
Bright, zest-infused, and deeply savory, this light pan-seared chicken features fresh citrus and herbs that cut through rich garlic butter, making it perfect for pairing with pasta, rice, or veggies.

* **Prep time:** 10 mins
* **Cook time:** 10 mins
* **Servings:** 3–4

#### Ingredients
* **1 lb** boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
* **2 tbsp** cornstarch
* **½ tsp** dried oregano (or Italian seasoning)
* **½ tsp** salt
* **¼ tsp** black pepper
* **1 tbsp** olive oil
* **4 tbsp** unsalted butter
* **4 cloves** fresh garlic, minced
* **2 tbsp** fresh lemon juice (plus lemon zest)
* **1 tbsp** fresh parsley, chopped

#### Instructions
1. **Coat:** Toss chicken pieces with cornstarch, oregano, salt, and black pepper.
2. **Sear:** Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken undisturbed for 3–4 minutes, then flip and cook another 4–5 minutes until golden.
3. **Sauce:** Turn heat down to low. Add butter, minced garlic, lemon juice, and zest. Stir for 1 minute until fragrant and glossy.
4. **Serve:** Toss in parsley and serve hot.

### 4. Crispy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Bites
Rich, comforting, and super crispy, these bites are coated in garlic butter and tossed in warm Parmesan cheese to create a nutty, irresistible golden crust.

* **Prep time:** 10 mins
* **Cook time:** 10 mins
* **Servings:** 3–4

#### Ingredients
* **1 lb** boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
* **2 tbsp** cornstarch
* **1 tsp** garlic powder
* **½ tsp** Italian seasoning
* **½ tsp** salt
* **¼ tsp** black pepper
* **1 tbsp** olive oil
* **3 tbsp** unsalted butter
* **4 cloves** fresh garlic, minced
* **⅓ cup** freshly grated Parmesan cheese
* **1 tbsp** fresh parsley, chopped

#### Instructions
1. **Coat:** Toss chicken with cornstarch, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
2. **Sear:** Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken in a single layer for 3–4 minutes, stir, and finish cooking for 4–5 minutes.
3. **Butter & Cheese:** Lower heat to medium-low. Add butter and garlic; cook for 1 minute. Turn off the heat completely, sprinkle in the Parmesan cheese, and toss quickly so it melts into a warm coating.
4. **Serve:** Sprinkle with parsley and serve.

### 5. Crispy Sweet Chili Garlic Chicken
Inspired by favorite takeout flavors, this quick skillet meal delivers crunchy chicken pieces tossed in a glossy, sweet, and mildly spicy Thai chili glaze.

* **Prep time:** 10 mins
* **Cook time:** 10 mins
* **Servings:** 3–4

#### Ingredients
* **1 lb** boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
* **2 tbsp** cornstarch
* **½ tsp** garlic powder
* **½ tsp** salt
* **1 tbsp** vegetable or sesame oil
* **2 tbsp** unsalted butter
* **3 cloves** fresh garlic, minced
* **⅓ cup** sweet Thai chili sauce
* **1 tbsp** soy sauce
* **1 tsp** sriracha *(optional, for extra heat)*
* **1 tbsp** sesame seeds & chopped green onions for garnish

#### Instructions
1. **Coat:** Toss chicken with cornstarch, garlic powder, and salt.
2. **Sear:** Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Cook chicken pieces for 3–4 minutes untouched, then stir and cook 4–5 minutes more.
3. **Sauce:** Reduce heat to medium-low. Add butter and garlic, cooking for 30 seconds. Pour in sweet chili sauce, soy sauce, and optional sriracha. Stir continuously for 1–2 minutes until sticky.
4. **Serve:** Top with sesame seeds and green onions.

### 6. Crispy Cajun Butter Chicken Bites
Packed with bold Southern kick, this recipe features seared, spiced chicken coated in a rich, garlic-infused Cajun butter sauce.

* **Prep time:** 10 mins
* **Cook time:** 10 mins
* **Servings:** 3–4

#### Ingredients
* **1 lb** boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
* **2 tbsp** cornstarch
* **1 tbsp** Cajun seasoning *(low sodium recommended)*
* **¼ tsp** black pepper
* **1 tbsp** olive oil
* **4 tbsp** unsalted butter
* **4 cloves** fresh garlic, minced
* **1 tbsp** fresh parsley or green onions, chopped

#### Instructions
1. **Coat:** Toss chicken with cornstarch, Cajun seasoning, and black pepper.
2. **Sear:** Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and sear for 3–4 minutes without moving, then stir and cook 4–5 minutes until done.
3. **Cajun Butter:** Reduce heat to medium-low. Melt butter in the skillet with minced garlic, stirring for about 1 minute until fragrant.
4. **Serve:** Remove from heat, toss with parsley, and serve immediately.
